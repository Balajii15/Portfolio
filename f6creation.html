How it works

Four stages, every project.

The same shape whether it's a 30-second sting or a full site build.

01

Brief

We talk through the goal, audience and constraints, and agree on scope, timeline and cost before anything starts.

02

Concept

Rough directions — sketches, moodboards or a wireframe — so we agree on the shape before the detail work begins.

03

Craft

The real build: animation, design, edit or code, with a couple of checkpoints so nothing drifts off-brief.

04

Ship

Final files, exports or a deployed site, plus source handoff — so it's yours to keep and reuse.

About

One person, the whole project.

Most studios split motion, design and development across different people and different handoffs. I do the animating, the designing and the building myself, which means fewer briefings lost in translation and a finished piece that looks like one idea, not three.

I work with founders, small teams and agencies who need something specific made well — a launch film, a brand refresh, a site that loads fast and looks like it was designed on purpose. Projects run from a single day to a few weeks, and I'm upfront about what's realistic in the time you've got.
